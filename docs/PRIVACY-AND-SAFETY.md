# Privacy and safety baseline

## Public promise

Rendezvue provides serious dating discovery without requiring users to publish the original face capture. Membership is adult-only and open to eligible singles; student status is optional.

## Data classes

1. account and authentication data;
2. eligibility and age evidence;
3. public profile and life stage;
4. optional student evidence;
5. family context;
6. religious-belief data;
7. highly sensitive source media;
8. derived public privacy portrait;
9. attraction signals, matches and private communications;
10. feedback, reports, moderation and audit evidence;
11. subscriptions, contact entitlements and payment events.

Each class requires a separate purpose, access policy, retention rule and deletion path.

## Family-data principles

- collect only relationship-relevant adult self-description;
- never collect names, photos, exact ages, schools or locations of children;
- child count is optional and coarse;
- divorce, widowhood and parenthood do not create hidden quality penalties;
- visibility is explicit and correctable;
- family data is not an advertising segment.

## Faith-data principles

- self-selected only;
- no inference from name, appearance, institution, location or behaviour;
- no piety score;
- self-description and preference remain separate;
- practice visibility starts private;
- field-level editing, deletion and withdrawal;
- no sale or advertising segmentation;
- DPIA/legal review before real use.

## Source-media and portrait principles

- request camera access only in context;
- never publish raw capture;
- require one explicit bounded privacy treatment before participant card/avatar registration;
- provide no raw, original or none option and do not preselect a level;
- bake the selected treatment into both public derivatives before upload;
- keep the normalized source private and redact all Storage paths from browser snapshots and audit payloads;
- process locally where feasible;
- use short-lived encrypted source storage only when necessary;
- delete source media automatically and retain deletion evidence;
- do not train models without separate optional consent;
- do not claim that blur provides anonymity;
- keep AI portrait generation optional rather than a prerequisite.

## Age, relationship and student principles

- age assurance is independent of student verification;
- student email is a dated probability/evidence signal, not identity proof;
- current single status is self-declared and periodically reconfirmed;
- suspected minors or concealed relationships enter a review workflow;
- significant decisions have an appeal route.

## Interaction and feedback principles

- mutual interest before contact;
- one contact right may open a conversation; safety is never paid;
- text-only first live MVP;
- pass and like are personal attraction signals;
- no public ratings or popularity scores;
- neutral incompatibility has no visibility effect;
- ordinary feedback and safety reports are separate;
- a single review cannot trigger distribution loss;
- serious reports create moderation cases;
- material restrictions are explainable and appealable.

## Prototype warning

The current repository is a synthetic browser concept pilot. It may save local demo progress but must not process real identity documents, production religious profiles, source-media archives, payments or real dating conversations. Real-user admission requires an external backend and explicit legal, security and moderation approval.
