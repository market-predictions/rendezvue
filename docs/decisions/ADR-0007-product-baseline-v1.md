# ADR-0007 — Product baseline v1 and student-first open membership

**Status:** accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

## Context

The earlier product admitted only Muslim students and treated a future AI-generated avatar as the production direction. Research and product discussion identified risks: insufficient local profile density, weak profitability, avoidable AI cost/latency, an overly administrative onboarding order, missing family context, unclear monetisation and no formal feedback model.

## Decision

1. Membership becomes adult, single and serious-intent based; student status is optional.
2. Students retain a verified badge, Campus Mode, events and discounted contact pricing as future benefits.
3. Life stage replaces student status as the general compatibility dimension.
4. Prior marital history, children, child wish and openness to a partner with children become separate profile fields.
5. Browser-local fuzzy privacy portraits are the MVP baseline; AI portraits are optional later experiments.
6. Registration, discovery, likes and receiving matches remain free; a contact entitlement may open a matched conversation and both parties can then reply.
7. Pass/like, private feedback, safety reports and trust signals remain separate.
8. The static concept pilot implements the full interaction loop locally; persistent multi-user operation requires an external server-authoritative backend.

## Consequences

- larger addressable and locally dense community;
- retained student differentiation without excluding non-students;
- less dependency on image-generation providers;
- more profile fields but a clearer progressive onboarding;
- monetisation is tested at the moment of demonstrated value;
- feedback cannot become a public popularity or moral score;
- current hosted build remains synthetic until backend, privacy and moderation gates pass.
